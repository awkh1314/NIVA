## Policy

The official NIVA character model, artwork, voice, brand assets, and custom commercial motion assets are not automatically covered by the software license of this repository. Their licensing must be defined separately before production release.

No GPL, unlicensed, or unclear-license binary asset should be added to the production asset directories without explicit review.
